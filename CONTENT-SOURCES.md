## Choices made for accuracy

- The eight Amity-derived cards were replaced with actual listed Sharda disciplines. They link to official programme pages. A keyword such as AI is a discovery aid, not a promise of a separately named AI Ph.D. or available seat.
- Listed programme specialisations are examples. The directory contains more disciplines, accessible through the “All doctoral disciplines” link.
- Programme pages show ₹75,000 tuition and ₹36,000 admission/exam fees but do not consistently specify the billing period. The redesign does not call the tuition annual, per-semester, or a total. Confirm the next-intake fee schedule.
- Fellowship is conditional. No Amity stipend, guaranteed waiver or invented Sharda award amount is used.
- The latest notice's bachelor's route includes research and indexed-publication conditions; it is not simplified to a marks-only eligibility claim. The complete official notice is linked.
- The study-mode wording does not equate part-time with distance learning, and does not promise an attendance-free Ph.D. The next intake may change arrangements.
- The three-year minimum is attributed to featured programme listings, not a guarantee of completion or a claim about every discipline.
- NAAC describes institutional accreditation; NBA applies to selected professional programmes. The site does not imply blanket NBA accreditation of doctoral programmes.
- No fabricated scholar testimonials, guaranteed placement percentages, response-time promises, live seat counts or application deadlines were introduced.

Review these facts whenever the university issues a new intake notice. Deployment and admissions-system integration were not part of this design pass.
