Admissions to Ph.D. programme (full-time and part-time) for batch July 2026 of Sharda University will be made strictly according to the UGC guidelines for NET, GATE & other equivalent test qualified candidates with valid score. Welcome to
Sharda University

Sharda University has over the years become one of the leading centres of education, research and innovation in Delhi NCR region. Established through an act of State Legislature of Uttar Pradesh (14 of 2009),

Sharda University offers over 135+ programmes in various disciplines. It's a part of the renowned Sharda Group with operations in areas like Education, Healthcare and IT.

Being the only global university in India with students from 95+ countries, and academic partnerships with 330+ Universities across the world-USA, UK, Italy, Canada, Russia, Slovenia, et al, the University offers a truly international learning environment.
